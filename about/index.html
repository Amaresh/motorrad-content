About Tyming Chain

Built around the workshop moments that usually break first.

Tyming Chain exists for the messy handoff points inside a real motorcycle garage: overbooked mornings, unclear job ownership, missing parts, delayed approvals, late pickups, and owners who only discover the problem after the customer already felt it.

Lens
Workflow

We care more about service flow than generic software feature checklists.

Audience
Operators

The product is built for people running and working inside motorcycle workshops.

Goal
Control

Make the next action obvious before the problem grows into customer pain.

Product principle

Visibility should travel from the customer desk to the bay floor and all the way to the owner’s view of the business.

What we optimise for

Cleaner decisions during the day, not prettier confusion.

The system should tell the staff what matters now: free the bay, follow up on the customer, chase the vendor, or move the invoice forward.

What matters most

Usability on the floor, clarity at the desk, and live signal for the owner.

If a tool only works for one of those roles, the workshop still falls back to parallel systems and memory.

Tyming Chain is designed for motorcycle workshops that want tighter workflow, better follow-through, and clearer owner visibility.
