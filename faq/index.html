Tyming Chain questions, answered in plain language

Direct answers for search and for people comparing bike garage software. Updated 7 Sep 2026.

Updated 7 Sep 2026

What is Tyming Chain?

Tyming Chain is bike garage management software for Indian two-wheeler workshops. It connects bookings, job cards, bays, parts, GST invoices, and WhatsApp follow-up.

Read the full module map or open job cards.

Is Tyming Chain a timing chain manufacturer or a repair tutorial site?

No. Tyming Chain is workshop software. It is not a maker of engine timing chains and it is not a how-to site for replacing them.

See why the product exists.

Does Tyming Chain generate GST invoices?

Yes. It builds GST-compliant invoices from the approved estimate, with CGST and SGST for intra-state work and IGST for inter-state, covering labour and spare parts.

Open GST billing or the GST invoice guide.

Can it stop overbooking repair bays?

Yes. Scheduling is against real bay time on 4 bays, with duration-aware slots (example: 12 weekday jobs, 8 on Saturday).

See bay scheduling and how to stop overbooking.

How do job cards work?

Each bike gets one auto-numbered job card from walk-in to delivery. Complaints, steps, parts, estimates, invoices, and timestamps attach to that record.

Read digital job cards for bike garages.

Does it send WhatsApp billing and service reminders?

Yes. Estimates and Cashfree payment links go out on WhatsApp, and service reminders fire by time or kilometres.

See WhatsApp reminders and billing.

Is it built for car garages or bike workshops?

It is built for Indian two-wheeler workshops — motorcycles and scooters — covering individual riders, corporate fleets, and delivery bikes, not as a generic car-dealer ERP.

Compare options on paper vs Excel vs auto ERP vs Tyming Chain.

Does inventory reorder from the job card?

Yes. Parts have reorder levels; hitting the floor flags a purchase order and receiving stock can unblock waiting job cards.

Open garage inventory management.

How do I start?

Use the contact page to book a setup walkthrough. The public site CTA is start free via that conversation.

Contact Tyming Chain to start.

How is this different from paper or Excel?

Paper and Excel do not hold bay time, GST invoices, WhatsApp approvals, and stock on one job card. See the comparison table.
