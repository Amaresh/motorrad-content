Account

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Motorrad Club account and associated personal data from inside the app. Motorrad Club is operated by Amaresh (individual).

In the app

  1. Open Settings from the drawer menu.
  2. Scroll to Account → tap Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and submit.

You will be signed out immediately. Deletion is processed on our servers and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Profile information (name, email, authentication records)
  • Ride history, telemetry, photos, and AI analysis tied to your account
  • Club chat messages and MotoBot history stored on our servers
  • Club Pro entitlement records on our servers (active Google Play subscriptions must also be cancelled in Play Store)

What may be retained

We may retain minimal logs or backups for a limited period where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Aggregated or de-identified analytics are not personal data.
