Club Pro

Subscription terms

Effective date: May 24, 2026 · Last updated: June 3, 2026

Club Pro is an optional paid subscription offered by Amaresh (individual) that unlocks premium features in the Motorrad Club Android app.

Billing

  • Payment is processed by Google Play. We do not store your full payment card details.
  • Prices, billing period, and trial terms (if offered) are shown in the Play Store purchase sheet before you confirm.
  • Subscription automatically renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period.

Features

Premium features may include advanced ride analytics, AI ride analysis, club chat, ride photos/memories, and other capabilities marked as Club Pro in the app. Feature availability may change as the product evolves.

Manage or cancel

  1. Open Google Play Store on your device.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Motorrad Club / Club Pro and choose Cancel.

Cancelling stops future charges. You keep access until the end of the paid period unless otherwise stated in Play Store.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Google under Google Play refund policies. Contact us at ambus1989@gmail.com if you need help locating your purchase.

Account deletion

Deleting your Motorrad Club account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel in Play Store separately. See Delete your account.
