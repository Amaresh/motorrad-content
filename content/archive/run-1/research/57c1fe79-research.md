## Key Statistics & Data Points
- Oil and brake fluid should be changed at least once per season.
- Air filters need replacement every 10,000-15,000 miles, or more often in dusty environments.
- Fork and shock servicing recommended every 2-3 seasons.
- Tire pressure and brake pad checks should be performed monthly or before every major ride.
- Using OEM filters and fluids is recommended for longevity and warranty compliance.
