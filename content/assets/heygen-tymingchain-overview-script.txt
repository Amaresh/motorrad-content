TYMING CHAIN — HEYGEN VIDEO SCRIPT

Recommended length: 75 to 90 seconds
Tone: Clear, confident, operator-focused
Audience: Motorcycle garage owners and workshop managers in India

Scene 1
On-screen text: Most garages do not have a software problem. They have a workflow problem.
Voiceover: Most motorcycle garages do not struggle because the team works hard. They struggle because bookings, job cards, parts, billing, and customer follow-up live in different places.

Scene 2
On-screen text: Tyming Chain connects the chain.
Voiceover: Tyming Chain is the operating system for modern motorcycle workshops. It connects the front desk, the workshop floor, the billing desk, and the owner dashboard in one workflow.

Scene 3
On-screen text: Plan bookings against real bay capacity.
Voiceover: Appointments are scheduled against real bay availability and service duration, so the day starts with a realistic plan instead of an overloaded promise.

Scene 4
On-screen text: Turn every arrival into a clean job card.
Voiceover: When the customer arrives, the booking becomes a ready-to-work job card with the complaint, vehicle details, service type, and workflow already attached.

Scene 5
On-screen text: Keep the floor moving.
Voiceover: Technicians and staff can see assigned work, live status, and the next step clearly. If a part is missing or approval is pending, the system flags the block early so the team can free the bay and move on.

Scene 6
On-screen text: Estimates, parts, billing, and reminders stay connected.
Voiceover: Estimates move into invoices without duplicate entry. Parts usage and stock levels stay visible. Customers get the right reminders and updates without your staff having to remember every follow-up manually.

Scene 7
On-screen text: See what is helping the business and what is slowing it down.
Voiceover: Owners can track turnaround time, revenue mix, blocked jobs, customer retention, technician workload, and payment status from the same operational trail.

Scene 8
On-screen text: Software for Indian motorcycle workshops.
Voiceover: If you want a cleaner workshop flow, sharper customer communication, and better visibility across the business, Tyming Chain is built for that exact job.

Closing frame
On-screen text: Tyming Chain
Subtext: Book a workshop walkthrough
CTA: tymingchain.com/contact
Voiceover: Visit tymingchain.com and book a walkthrough.
