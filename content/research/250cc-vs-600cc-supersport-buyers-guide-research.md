## Key Statistics & Data Points
- 250cc supersports weigh 30-50 kg less than 600cc models, offering easier handling for new riders.
- 600cc supersports deliver 2-3x the horsepower (~110hp vs ~40hp for 250cc).
- Insurance premiums for 600cc bikes are 2-3x higher than 250cc, especially for riders under 25.
- 250cc fuel economy averages 60-80 mpg vs 35-45 mpg for 600cc models.
- Modern 600cc supersports now include switchable ABS, traction control, and ride modes as standard in 2026.
- Annual maintenance costs average $300-500 for 250cc vs $600-1,000 for 600cc due to tire and consumable differences.
