## Key Statistics & Data Points
- Oil and brake fluid should be changed at least once per season for sport bikes.
- Fork and shock servicing is recommended every 2-3 seasons.
- Air filters need replacement every 10,000-15,000 miles (more often in dusty conditions).
- OEM filters and manufacturer-specified oil weights are recommended for longevity and warranty compliance.
- Leak inspections (fork seals, coolant, oil) should be part of every service intake.
- Garages using digital maintenance tracking report up to 25% higher customer retention.
