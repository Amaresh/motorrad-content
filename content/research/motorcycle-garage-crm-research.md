## Content Angles
1. **Pain Points of Running Without Software:** How manual processes cost motorcycle garages time, money, and customers — missed appointments, lost service history, inventory chaos.
2. **CRM as a Sales Engine:** How tracking customer interactions and purchase history drives repeat business and upsells for motorcycle workshops.
3. **Service Scheduling & Workflow Optimization:** Automating appointment booking, technician assignments, and service reminders to eliminate bottlenecks.
4. **Parts Inventory Control:** Preventing overstock, shortages, and lost revenue with real-time inventory tracking integrated into CRM.
5. **From Dealership to Independent Garage:** Why CRM isn't just for big dealers — small and mid-size motorcycle garages benefit even more.
