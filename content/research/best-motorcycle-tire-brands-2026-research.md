## 3. Brand Analysis: Performance Data

### 3.1 Premium Tier — Independent Test Results

| Tire | Dry Lap (s) | Wet Lap (s) | Combined Score | Price (rear) | Est. Mileage |
|------|------------|------------|----------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Pirelli Supercorsa SP V4** | 91.2 | 109.8 | 94/100 | $280 | ~1,500 mi |
| **Michelin Power GP2** | 91.5 | 103.4 | 93/100 | $260 | ~2,800 mi |
| **Dunlop Sportmax Q5** | 91.8 | 104.1 | 91/100 | $240 | ~3,000 mi |
| **Bridgestone Battlax S23** | 92.1 | 104.8 | 89/100 | $230 | ~3,200 mi |
| **Continental SportAttack 4** | 92.4 | 103.9 | 88/100 | $220 | ~3,500 mi |
| **Metzeler Sportec M9 RR** | 92.0 | 105.2 | 87/100 | $225 | ~3,000 mi |
| **Michelin Power 6** | 92.8 | 102.1 | 90/100 | $235 | ~4,500 mi |
| **Pirelli Diablo Rosso IV** | 92.3 | 103.6 | 89/100 | $210 | ~4,000 mi |

### 3.2 Key Findings from Test Data

1. **Sport tires beat hypersport on combined scores** — Michelin Power GP2 and Dunlop Q5 dominate because wet performance gaps are massive (6–11 seconds)
2. **Pirelli Supercorsa V4 is fastest in dry** but suffers in rain — great for track, risky for street
3. **Michelin Power 6 is the longevity king** — 4,500+ miles with only 1.6s slower dry lap than Supercorsa
4. **Continental SportAttack 4 is the value play** — cheapest premium tire, 3,500 mi life, solid wet grip
5. **Bridgestone S23 punches above its price** — $230 gets you within 1s of the Supercorsa in dry
